Youth Savings

The National Social Security Fund (NSSF), together with the World Bank under the NYOTA Project, is helping young people save, plan for the future, and improve financial security. Through the NSSF Haba Haba savings wallet, you can start building good money habits, get incentives, and access special benefits like maternity support for female participants.

NYOTA Beneficiaries Registration

All NYOTA Project beneficiaries are automatically enrolled in the NSSF Haba Haba savings scheme. Beneficiaries do not need to go through a separate registration process; their enrollment into NSSF is seamless and automatic.

NYOTA Beneficiaries Youth Savings

Business Support

A beneficiary who qualifies for Business Support will receive KES 25,000 in two payments (tranches) to support their business.

Automatic Deduction: KES 3,000 goes directly to the NSSF Haba Haba savings wallet for each tranche.

On-the-Job Experience

If you join the On-the-Job Experience program, you will receive a monthly stipend of KES 6,000 for six months.

  • KES 720 goes directly to the NSSF Haba Haba savings wallet each month.
  • This means you are earning while saving and building your financial safety net.

The beneficiaries savings will be split into 30% Short-Term Savings and 70% Long-Term Savings.

Rules for Accessing Savings

  • 30% Short-Term Savings: Accessible 1 year after you start saving.
  • 70% Long-Term Savings: 50% is available after 5 years, and the rest is saved for your future life goals.

You can track your savings digitally, check your balance anytime, and add extra money to your Haba Haba account to grow your savings faster.

Matching Contributions

For every KES 2 you voluntarily save, the program provides a matching contribution of KES 1, giving you a 2:1 savings match.

  • Save KES 1,000 and receive KES 500 as a matching contribution, giving you KES 1,500.
  • Save KES 6,000 and receive KES 3,000 as a matching contribution, giving you KES 9,000.

To benefit from the matching contribution, save consistently for at least six months.

Maternity Benefit for Female Youth

Female youth participating in the savings program can opt in to the maternity benefit through USSD code *254#.

  • Notify NSSF during the second trimester through *254#.
  • The maternity premium is KES 400 per month for four months, totaling KES 1,600.
  • The maternity benefit is KES 4,000 per month for four months, totaling KES 16,000.
  • In the event of a stillbirth after seven months, the benefit applies once the case has been verified.

The maternity benefit is designed to provide financial support during the postnatal period.
