Youth Savings

The National Social Security Fund (NSSF), together with the World Bank under the NYOTA Project, is helping young people save, plan for the future, and improve financial security. Through the NSSF Haba Haba savings wallet, you can start building good money habits, get incentives, and access special benefits like maternity support for female participants.

NYOTA Beneficiaries Registration

All NYOTA Project beneficiaries are automatically enrolled in the NSSF Haba Haba savings scheme. Beneficiaries do not need to go through a separate registration process; their enrollment into NSSF is seamless and automatic.
