About NYOTA Project

The National Youth Opportunities Towards Advancement (NYOTA) project is a landmark initiative by the Government of Kenya, funded by the World Bank, aimed at transforming the lives of vulnerable youth. Over five years, NYOTA will create employment opportunities, enhance income generation, and promote savings among the youth population.

Project Development Objectives

To increase employment, earnings and promote savings among the targeted youth.

State Department for Youth Affairs and Creative Economy

The State Department of Youth Affairs and Creative Economy (SDYACE) is the coordinating agency of all interventions in the project, with the main objective of ensuring that NYOTA objectives and their implementation align with the government's agenda on youth employment. The State Department will implement a market-driven technical skills development aspect through On-the-Job Experience to prepare the youth for certification. It is also charged with monitoring and evaluating project deliverables to track and advise on project progress.

National Industrial Training Authority

NITA will assure quality standards are adhered to and provide certification to the project beneficiaries on apprenticeship.

National Employment Authority

Using the Kenya Market Observatory, the Kenya Labor Market Information System (KLMIS) will be fully operationalized to provide valuable information on employment outlook, job profiles, and career prospects, thereby providing real-time labour market information for career pathways and progression.

State Department for Micro, Small and Medium Enterprise Development

The State Department will provide digital training to targeted youth on AGPO and Catalytic funds that include YEDF, WEF, Uwezo Fund and FI.

National Social Security Fund

Beneficiaries are encouraged to save through monetary incentives, nudges and income replacement at maternity (insurance). This will be managed by NSSF through the Haba Haba scheme.

Micro and Small Enterprises Authority

MSEA implements the Expanding Employment Opportunities intervention under the NYOTA Project, providing comprehensive business support to equip youth with the skills and resources they need to build sustainable enterprises.

Kenya Labour Market Information System

Using the Kenya Market Observatory, the Kenya Labor Market Information System (KLMIS) will be fully operationalized to provide valuable information on employment outlook, job profiles, and career prospects, thereby providing real-time labour market information for career pathways and progression.
