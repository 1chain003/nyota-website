Improving Youth Entrepreneurship

This intervention aims to expand employment opportunities for young people by strengthening their entrepreneurial capacity and supporting the growth of sustainable youth-led enterprises. This is achieved through business development training, provision of business capital, mentorship, and market linkages to help youth transform their ideas into successful businesses.

Implementation Status

NYOTA has successfully implemented Business Development Services (BDS) training across 46 counties, equipping thousands of young people with foundational business skills, financial literacy, and enterprise management knowledge.

In the Western Kenya cluster, the Project reached a major milestone with the first disbursement of business capital. During the event, KSh 267 million was disbursed to 12,155 youth from Kakamega, Bungoma, Busia, and Vihiga counties. These beneficiaries had successfully completed the BDS training and qualified for financial support under the support entrepreneurship intervention. They represent the first cohort of young entrepreneurs to access business capital under the Project’s national rollout.

Following the disbursement, all beneficiaries will receive business mentorship from qualified business development experts to help them establish, strengthen, and grow their enterprises.

Key Interventions

Business Development Services

Beneficiaries will undergo comprehensive business skills training designed to equip them with the knowledge and tools needed to start, manage, and grow successful enterprises.

Business Capital

The Project provides financial support to help youth launch viable business ventures. This includes grants, and other forms of seed capital tailored to the enterprise’s nature and growth potential.

Mentorship & Coaching

Beneficiaries receive structured mentorship from experienced entrepreneurs and sector experts. This support helps them refine business models, strengthen operations, and navigate early-stage challenges.
