Capacity Build Youth to Transact with Government

The NYOTA project provides targeted interventions/opportunities to address specific challenges faced by vulnerable youth in Kenya.

Capacity Build Youth to Transact with Government

Conduct capacity building of youth on catalytic funds like Youth Enterprise Development Fund, Women Enterprise Fund, Uwezo Fund, Hustler Fund and AGPO for increased uptake of more than 5% provision on tenders.

Support to Government's Investment on Youth Employment Creation

Enhancing government capacities, NYOTA Project works closely with national and county governments to enhance their capacity to invest in youth employment initiatives through support to policy development, gender inclusion and climate change initiatives.
